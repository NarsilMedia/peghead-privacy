Peghead — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Peghead ("the app") is a guitar tuner. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is used. In short: the sound from your microphone never leaves your device, and the only data collected off-device is what our advertising provider needs to show ads.

Microphone

With your permission, Peghead listens through your device's microphone to measure the pitch of the note you play. The audio is analysed live, on your device, and immediately discarded. Peghead never records, stores, or transmits audio, and never shares it with anyone, including our advertising provider. You can turn off microphone access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Your tunings and settings

Custom tunings you save and your preferences (such as the A4 reference pitch) are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to any server.

Advertising

Peghead displays ads through Google AdMob. To do this, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier), usage and interaction data, and diagnostic information, and may use them to select and measure ads. You can read Google's practices at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices about tracking and consent

On first launch the app asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether apps may track you. If you are in the UK, EU, or another region that requires it, the app also shows a Google consent form and respects your choice; you can change it any time from the app's Settings under "Manage ad consent." Declining means you may see non-personalised ads or, where required, no ads.

Children

Peghead is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
