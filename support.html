Peghead — Support

Peghead is a guitar tuner: play a string and it tells you which one it is and whether to tune up or down.

Frequently asked

The tuner isn't reacting when I play.
Check Peghead has microphone access (Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Peghead). Hold the phone near the guitar's sound hole or amp, play one string at a time, and let it ring.

It's showing the wrong string.
In Guitar mode Peghead picks the string closest to the note you play, so a string that's far out of tune can look like its neighbour. Tap the string you're tuning to lock onto it, then tap "Locked · tap for auto" to go back to automatic.

How do I use an alternate tuning?
Tap the tuning name at the top of the screen and choose one, such as Drop D, DADGAD or Open G. To make your own, tap "New custom tuning", set each string, and save. Swipe left on a custom tuning to edit or delete it.

How do I hear the target note?
Tap any string. If you hear nothing, check the speaker button at the top of the screen is on and your phone isn't on silent.

My piano or a recording is a little off from the tuner.
Change the A4 reference in Settings (430–450 Hz) to match it.
